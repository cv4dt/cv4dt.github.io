Hi! I'm Markus, a second-year Ph.D. student at the Computer Vision Group of the Technical University of Munich (TUM), advised by Prof. Dr. Daniel Cremers; a research associate at the Autonomous Aerial Systems Group of the Fraunhofer Institute; and an affiliated researcher at the Munich Center for Machine Learning (MCML). Prior to that, I obtained the Master of Science in Computer Science with highest distinction from the Munich University of Applied Sciences.

My research interests include computer vision and machine learning, with their applications in mobile robotics, specifically autonomous flying and driving. I am also passionate about planetary robotics and spaceflight in general!

With the CV4DT I work on pushing forward the boundaries of UAV benchmarks for solving aerial visual understanding!
